A make.fun entry for [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca): a program that writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt` — a warning to the writer's past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

The moment is a Christmas Eve phone call waved off at the kitchen sink; the second line traces what that one small refusal cost every Christmas after it.

Run it with `node main.ts` (Node 22+). It writes `entry.txt` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
